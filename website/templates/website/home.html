Steps to Follow

1

STEP 1: SUBMIT YOUR IDEA

Submit your idea online. Registration opens on 15 JAN 2026. No fee is collected at this stage.

📅 Registration opens on 15 JAN 2026

Ensure you have the following ready:

• Team names and mobile numbers (Team Size: 4–6 members)

• A PPT report as per the given template (Download PPT Template)

• A 3-minute YouTube video link of your idea

2

STEP 2: EVALUATION AND SHORTLISTING

All submitted ideas will be evaluated by our expert panel, and shortlisted teams will be finalized based on innovation and feasibility.

📅 Shortlisting results: 20 JAN 2026

Keep checking your registered email inbox (and spam folder) for the acceptance notification.

3

STEP 3: REGISTRATION FOR SHORTLISTED TEAMS

Shortlisted teams must confirm their participation by completing the registration process and paying the applicable fee.

📅 Complete registration before 31 JAN 2026 (before the hackathon begins)

Registration Fee: ₹600 per student

Fee covers T-shirts and food arrangements (lunch, dinner, tea, and snacks during hackathon days).

4

STEP 4: HACKATHON IMPLEMENTATION

Implement and present your idea during the 36-hour on-campus hackathon. Be ready to showcase your project and compete for top prizes!

📅 Hackathon: 31 JAN 2026 – 01 FEB 2026

36 hours of coding, mentoring, demos, and the grand finale on 01 FEB 2026 at 7:00 PM.
